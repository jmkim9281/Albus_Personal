Tech Mahindra is one of India’s largest B2B IT service companies, part of the Mahindra Group. This project was a $5M global user research program for an enterprise-level IT client.

The situation and the challenge

An enterprise-level IT client was running a worldwide user study for its face-authentication security features. The Korea site needed new researchers onboarded quickly, consistent sessions with participants, and a steady flow of participants. A separate recruitment vendor was not adequately delivering any of these requirements.

Who was involved

Client headquartersTech Mahindra site leadResearch associatesStudy participantsParticipant recruitment vendor

What I did

  1. Apr 2023Joined the Korea site as a research associate
  2. PlaybookWrote the onboarding standard for new researchers
  3. Jul 2023Promoted to lead project assistant
  4. Nov 2023Contract completed
  • Ran sessions and analyzed the results, reporting insights for face-authentication-based security features to the client's headquarters on a regular cadence.
  • Wrote the onboarding playbook for incoming research associates: how to receive participants, run a session, and answer participant questions within the contract's limits.
  • Kept recruitment moving when the recruitment vendor fell short, by bringing in participants through the team's own network.

Key decisions

  • Write the playbook instead of training each new hire one by one.
    Sessions had to be consistent and confidential. A written standard made every researcher answer the same way, including on questions the contract did not allow us to answer.
  • Recruit participants ourselves rather than wait for the vendor.
    The schedule mattered more than whose job recruitment was. Waiting would have delayed the Korea data for the whole program.

What I took from it

In research, the schedule depends on the least visible parts: onboarding, recruitment, the answers people give participants. Writing those down was the most useful thing I did.
